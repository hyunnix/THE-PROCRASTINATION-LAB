03 / A NOTE FROM THE LAB

WHAT DOES THIS MODEL
ACTUALLY SHOW?

This simulation simplifies procrastination into three adjustable variables. Real procrastination is more complex and may involve emotions, context, habits, and individual differences. The readiness score is an illustrative output, not a measurement of a person's actual psychology.

UNDER THE HOOD / THE RULE

50 + (motivation × 6)
   − (difficulty × 4)
   − (distraction × 4)

Keep the result between 0 and 100.
A design choice, not a psychological formula.

The computational thinking behind it +
Decomposition
Break the phenomenon into difficulty, motivation, and distraction.
Pattern recognition
Compare combinations. In this model, more motivation raises readiness; more difficulty or distraction lowers it.
Abstraction
Represent a complex experience with three numbers and three visual states.
Algorithm design
Read inputs, calculate, clamp, choose a state, and update the screen. Repeat after each change.
